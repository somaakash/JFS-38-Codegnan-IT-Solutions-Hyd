Vijaywada


Vijayawada, located on the banks of the Krishna River, is one of the most beautiful cities in Andhra Pradesh. It is known for its rich culture, historic temples, and scenic landscapes.

  2. Prakkasam Bridge
  3. Bhavni Island
  4. Kapleshwar Temple
